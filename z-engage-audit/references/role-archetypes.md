# Role archetypes

The four role archetypes used by ZeeEngage. Every Strategy Artifact must classify the role into one of these. If a role fits two archetypes, ask the user which to use.

---

## Senior leadership

**Definition.** Director-level and above. Owns scope across functions or geographies. Reports to C-suite. Hires include VP Engineering, Head of Sales, Sr. Director Supply Chain, Chief of Staff.

**Signal hierarchy in the JD.**
1. Scope (geography, regions, functions owned)
2. Leadership scale (direct reports, total team size)
3. Cross-functional mandate (named peer functions)
4. Named customers or partners (if any)
5. Stage and ownership level (P&L, function, vertical)

**Channel mix.**

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Primary outreach; scope and impact framing |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn InMail (paid credit) | Second touch; SOBO-style if possible |
| 3 | 10 | Email | Reframe with new angle or named-customer context |
| 4 | 17 | LinkedIn breakup | Pattern-interrupt close |

**Do not** use SMS for senior leadership outreach. It reads as intrusive. SMS is acceptable late-stage only with explicit consent (e.g., "Can I text you scheduling options?").

**Tone.** Strategic, impact-driven. Position the role at its highest level (mini-COO, mini-CEO of function, etc.). The candidate asks: what will I lead and who will I influence?

**Screening question pattern.**
- Scope confirmation (have you owned X scale)
- Cross-functional experience (have you worked directly with Y peers)
- P&L or budget ownership (if applicable)
- Geographic or regulatory experience (if role is multi-region)
- Open-ended: what would your first 90 days look like

**Length.** Initial email 100 to 140 words. Executive variant may extend to 160.

**Benchmark targets (2026, internal).**

- Response rate (72h): 8 to 12%
- Screening completion: 50 to 65%
- Qualified routing: 65 to 75%

## Engineering / technical

**Definition.** Software engineers, ML engineers, infrastructure, DevOps, QA, hardware, network engineers. Includes IC roles from junior through staff/principal. Technical leadership (engineering managers, architects) often sits here, not in senior leadership, unless org-level scope is involved.

**Signal hierarchy in the JD.**
1. Tech stack (languages, frameworks, infra, named tools)
2. Domain (payments, networking, ML, security, etc.)
3. Type of work (build vs operate vs research)
4. Scale or system characteristics (throughput, latency, fleet size)
5. Team or product context (what's being built)

**Channel mix.**

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Stack and domain-specific outreach |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn InMail | Second touch; same stack signals |
| 3 | 10 | Email | Stack-specific reframe or technical detail |
| 4 | 17 | Email (or SMS if consent obtained) | Breakup |

**Tone.** Direct, technical, specific. Name the tools and the work. The candidate asks: what will I build and what will I use?

**Banned in engineering outreach.** Marketing language ("cutting-edge", "innovative", "next-generation", "exciting opportunity"). Engineers downgrade trust on every instance.

**Screening question pattern.**
- Stack proficiency (have you worked with X in production)
- Scale experience (have you operated systems at Y scale)
- Domain knowledge (do you have exposure to Z protocols/patterns)
- Specific must-have from JD (e.g., "Have you led HLD/LLD for IP/MPLS edge")
- Environment fit (Linux, on-call expectations, etc.)

**Length.** Initial email 75 to 110 words. Compact variant 60 to 80.

**Benchmark targets (2026, internal).**

- Response rate (72h): 6 to 10%
- Screening completion: 55 to 70%
- Qualified routing: 60 to 75%

## Sales / commercial

**Definition.** Account executives, BDR/SDR, sales engineers, customer success, channel partners. Quota-carrying or quota-adjacent.

**Signal hierarchy in the JD.**
1. Territory or segment (named geography, vertical, account list)
2. Quota and OTE (where disclosed)
3. Sales motion (inbound, outbound, channel, enterprise)
4. Product or category being sold
5. Career progression signal (promotion path, equity, ramp time)

**Channel mix.**

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Territory and earnings framing |
| 2 | 3 | SMS (if number available) | Second touch; salespeople respond fast to text |
| 3 | 10 | Email | Numbers-led reframe (quota, OTE, growth rate) |
| 4 | 17 | LinkedIn breakup | Close the loop |

**Tone.** Growth-oriented, revenue-focused. Reference territory, market opportunity, and earning potential where data is available. The candidate asks: what can I earn and what is the upside?

**Screening question pattern.**
- Quota attainment history
- Average deal size and cycle length
- Sales motion fit (do you sell to X persona, in Y industry)
- Hunter vs farmer fit
- Compensation expectations vs disclosed range

**Length.** Initial email 75 to 110 words. SMS under 160 characters.

**Benchmark targets (2026, internal).**

- Response rate (72h): 10 to 15%
- Screening completion: 60 to 75%
- Qualified routing: 55 to 70%

## Operations / functional

**Definition.** Operations managers, supply chain, HR, finance ops, project managers, program managers, non-engineering technical (e.g., data analysts who are not engineers). Mid-level individual contributors and managers.

**Signal hierarchy in the JD.**
1. Scope of ownership (process, function, geography)
2. Cross-functional partners (named peer functions)
3. Team size (if managing)
4. Domain or industry context
5. Specific tools or systems (ERP, ATS, financial systems)

**Channel mix.**

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Email | Scope and ownership framing |
| 2 | 3 | SMS (transactional framing, with opt-out) | Second touch |
| 3 | 10 | Email | Reframe with team or process detail |
| 4 | 17 | SMS reminder | Final touch |

**Tone.** Process and ownership-focused. Reference scope, geography, team size, and autonomy. The candidate asks: what will I own and how much autonomy will I have?

**Screening question pattern.**
- Functional experience (have you owned X process/function)
- Scope confirmation (geography, team size, budget)
- Tool or system experience (have you worked with Y ERP/ATS/etc.)
- Cross-functional partner experience
- Specific JD must-haves

**Length.** Initial email 75 to 120 words.

**Benchmark targets (2026, internal).**

- Response rate (72h): 8 to 12%
- Screening completion: 55 to 70%
- Qualified routing: 60 to 70%

## Cross-archetype rules

**Channel selection by generation (where inferable from disclosed role seniority, never from name or photo).**

- Gen Z candidates (early-career signals): SMS as touch 2 even for engineering roles; informal language; never call without prior text.
- Millennial candidates: email plus SMS standard; structured but conversational.
- Gen X candidates: email-first, longer-form acceptable; no SMS unless candidate initiates.

**Send-time defaults (recipient timezone).**

- Email: Tuesday 9am, Wednesday 9am, Thursday 9am
- LinkedIn InMail: Tuesday or Wednesday 11am
- SMS: Wednesday or Thursday 11am
- Passive senior leadership: Sunday 2pm has shown strong engagement in 2025 to 2026 Gem data

**Compliance overlay.** All archetypes require EU AI Act disclosure (for EU-based candidates), GDPR Article 22 right-to-contest, and NYC Local Law 144 (for NYC-based candidates and roles). See references/compliance.md.
